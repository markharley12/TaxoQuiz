Wikidata and Wikipedia are different places

Wikidata is a database: one item per species, with an ID like Q140, holding facts as separate statements (scientific name, rank, parent taxon, common names, a photo). TaxoQuiz builds its whole tree from it. A wrong name, a wrong parent, a species listed twice or a group word as a common name is a Wikidata problem, and fixing it there fixes it for every language at once.

Wikipedia is the encyclopedia, a separate wiki per language. TaxoQuiz takes only the popup from it: the summary and the infobox picture. A popup about the wrong animal or a range map instead of a photo is a Wikipedia problem, unless the Wikidata item links to the wrong article.

Behind both sit the taxonomic authorities, specialist lists for each group such as the Reptile Database. Where Wikidata is ambiguous, they are the closest thing to a source of truth, and the Wikidata item usually links to them.
